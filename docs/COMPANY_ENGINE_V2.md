# NOVARCH Company Engine v2

Status: build branch

## Product principle
novarch.eu is not an offer page. It is NOVARCH's public operating surface: positioning, proof, conversion, launches, community entry, talent, advisors, campaigns and transactions.

## Core narrative
NOVARCH builds intelligent systems around real work. We combine applied AI, workflow architecture and accountable human control to turn fragmented operations into systems people and organizations can actually own and improve.

The commercial entry point stays concrete: expensive repeated workflows. The brand is intentionally broader: businesses, institutions and eventually NOVARCH products/private AI systems.

## Evidence architecture
Claims on the public site must be evidence-led. Current approved trails:
- Founder AI work beginning in 2018-19.
- Orbit Syndicate.
- GlobeDigits.
- The Retail Cube.
- RaabtaDesk founder/product trail.
- German institutional trail around Ilmenau, systems acquisition, privacy/data protection and related university workstreams.

Do not inflate evidence into client, funding, institutional-partnership or outcome claims unless separately substantiated for that exact wording.

## Website architecture
### 1. Home
Fast comprehension + ambition + proof + conversion.
- Hero: intelligent systems around real work.
- System thesis / workflow visual.
- Proof rail.
- Three doors: Business / Institutions / Products.
- Selected work.
- Ownership manifesto.
- Launch/waitlist surface.
- Primary commercial CTA.

### 2. Systems
Explain what NOVARCH builds without reducing the company to an automation agency.
- Workflow systems.
- Private/owned AI systems.
- Institutional systems.
- Productized systems as they become real.

### 3. Work / Proof
Evidence library rather than logo theatre.
Each case can carry: context, NOVARCH/founder responsibility, system/problem, evidence type, and what it taught the current thesis.

### 4. Company
Founder story, team, Germany base, international operating history, institutional trail and current direction.

### 5. Manifesto
Human agency, ownership, privacy, continuity, accountable AI and opportunity.

### 6. Launch
Reusable campaign surface for waitlists, product launches and experiments. Source/referral aware.

### 7. Community
A deliberate entry surface; do not pretend an inactive community is mature. Can initially route to an interest list/community application.

### 8. Talent
Evergreen hiring pipeline for builders/operators. Roles can be added independently.

### 9. Advisors
Explain the advisor network NOVARCH wants and provide a clean expression-of-interest path. Named advisors only after approval.

### 10. Pay
Existing secure customer payment surface remains isolated and regression-protected.

## Conversion hierarchy
1. Start a workflow / commercial conversation.
2. Join a relevant launch or product waitlist.
3. Explore proof.
4. Join community / talent / advisor pipelines.
5. Pay only through a reference-linked customer flow.

## Build rules
- Beam-level product ambition, NOVARCH identity.
- No fake maturity and no self-deprecation.
- Proof before adjectives.
- Modular routes so campaigns can be added without redesigning the core site.
- Preserve working payment routes and backend contract.
- Privacy-aware forms with explicit purpose/source fields.
- Mobile-first acceptance required before production promotion.
